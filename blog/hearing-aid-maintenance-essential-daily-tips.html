Hearing Aid Maintenance: Essential Daily Tips

1. The 2-Minute Daily Care Protocol

Follow this nightly sequence every time you remove your hearing aids before sleep:

Nightly Checklist

  • Wipe Down with Microfiber Cloth: Gently clean the entire casing and dome to remove body oils and perspiration. Never use alcohol wipes or household solvents.
  • Brush the Microphone Ports: Hold the instrument microphone-side facing down and gently brush ports so dislodged debris falls away rather than inward.
  • Inspect the Wax Guard (CeruStop): Check the tiny white filter at the tip of the receiver. If clogged with orange/brown wax, replace it with a fresh tool.
  • Store in a Dehumidifying Case: Place devices in a dedicated electronic dry box or desiccant capsule to extract destructive internal moisture.

2. Fast Fixes for Common In-Home Problems

  • No Sound (Dead Aid): Check if wax is blocking the receiver filter (replace wax guard); check battery charge or ensure contacts are clean.
  • Whistling / Squealing: Ensure dome is seated fully inside canal; check if ears have cerumen impaction causing sound to bounce back into the microphone.
  • Muffled or Distorted Sound: Likely moisture accumulation. Run through electronic drying cycle for 4 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I wear my hearing aids in the shower or swimming pool?

No. Even water-resistant IP68-rated hearing aids are designed for accidental splashes or rain, not pressurized immersion, hot steam, or chlorinated water.
