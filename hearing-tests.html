Diagnostic Precision

Why Clinical Diagnostic Hearing Tests are Essential

Hearing loss rarely occurs overnight. In the vast majority of cases, it develops gradually, subtly diminishing higher frequencies first. People frequently compensate by turning up television volume, asking others to repeat phrases, or feeling fatigued after conversational dinners.

A clinical hearing test in Bengaluru at SoundBridge Clinic provides an objective, scientific mapping of your auditory thresholds. Rather than guessing, our audiologists determine precisely which sound frequencies are affected, whether the root cause is in the outer/middle ear (conductive) or the inner ear cochlea and auditory nerve (sensorineural), and what remediation strategy will yield the greatest communicative improvement.

Audiometric Assessments

Comprehensive Battery of Hearing Assessments Offered

Depending on patient age, reported symptoms, and medical history, our clinical testing battery includes:

Pure Tone Audiometry (PTA)

Measures air and bone conduction thresholds across 250 Hz to 8000 Hz to generate your official diagnostic audiogram graph.

Speech Audiometry (SRT & SDS)

Evaluates speech recognition threshold and word discrimination scores in both quiet and simulated noisy listening conditions.

Tympanometry & Reflexes

Tests middle ear pressure, eardrum mobility, and acoustic stapedial reflexes to detect fluid, glue ear, or eustachian tube dysfunction.

Pediatric Hearing Tests

Conditioned Play Audiometry (CPA) and Visual Reinforcement Audiometry (VRA) tailored specifically for toddlers and young children.

Clinical Protocol

Step-by-Step Clinical Hearing Consultation Process

At SoundBridge Clinic, we understand that medical appointments can cause apprehension. Our clinical process is designed to be comfortable, thorough, and completely transparent:

  1. Clinical History & Otoscopic Examination: We review your medical history, noise exposure, and communication difficulties, followed by a video otoscopic inspection of the outer ear canal to check for earwax impaction, perforation, or inflammation.
  2. Testing in the Soundproof Chamber: You will be seated inside our soundproof booth wearing calibrated insert earphones or circumaural headphones. You simply press a button or raise your hand every time you perceive a tone, even if very faint.
  3. Bone Conduction Testing: A gentle vibrating oscillator is placed against the mastoid bone behind your ear to bypass the outer and middle ear, directly testing the cochlear sensory nerve.
  4. Speech Clarity Measurement: You repeat two-syllable and single-syllable phonetically balanced words to evaluate word discrimination percentage.
  5. Detailed Audiogram Interpretation & Counseling: Our audiologist explains your audiogram results in clear, non-technical language. If hearing loss is identified, we present honest options—whether medical referral, monitoring, or digital hearing aid trials.

Preventing Auditory Deprivation

When auditory nerves stop receiving sound stimulation over years, the auditory cortex in the brain gradually loses its ability to decipher speech clarity—a phenomenon called auditory deprivation. Early testing and intervention maintain brain auditory pathways active and sharp.
