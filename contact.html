Transit Routes

How to Reach SoundBridge Clinic from Nearby Areas & Mysuru

  • From Nagarbhavi 2nd Stage / BDA Complex: Head west along Muddinapalya Main Road toward D-Group Layout. We are located in Sri Anantha Kausthubha, 1st Floor (approx. 5-7 minutes drive).
  • From Vijayanagar & Chandra Layout: Take the Nagarbhavi Main Road / Outer Ring Road connecting toward Muddinapalya (approx. 10-15 minutes).
  • From Kengeri Satellite Town & RR Nagar: Travel via Ullal Main Road or Outer Ring Road toward Nagarbhavi D-Group Layout (approx. 12-18 minutes).
  • For Patients Traveling from Mysuru (Mysore), Mandya & Ramanagara: SoundBridge regularly welcomes families and senior citizens from Mysuru seeking specialized pediatric speech evaluations and multi-brand digital hearing aid trials. Take the Bengaluru-Mysuru Expressway (NH 275) to the Kengeri/NICE Road exit, then proceed via Ullal Main Road to Nagarbhavi (approx. 90–100 minutes total drive). Kengeri Railway Station is just 15 minutes away by taxi.
  • Public Transport: Multiple BMTC buses operate regularly between Majestic (KBS) / Vijayanagar and Muddinapalya / D-Group Layout with bus stops nearby.
