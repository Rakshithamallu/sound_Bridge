Preventive Audiological Care

Why Prompt Hearing Aid Maintenance Matters

Hearing aids operate in one of the harshest environments imaginable: inside the human ear canal, constantly exposed to body heat, moisture, sweat, and earwax (cerumen). Over months of daily use, wax particles clog acoustic filters and receiver sound bores, while humidity can corrode miniature internal circuitry.

At SoundBridge Speech & Hearing Clinic, our repair department provides rapid diagnostic troubleshooting, specialized cleaning, electronic component testing, and genuine manufacturer repair coordination for all major digital hearing aid brands across Bengaluru.

We Service All Major Global Hearing Aid Brands

Whether you wear Signia, Phonak, Widex, ReSound, Starkey, Oticon, or Unitron, we have the specialized programming software, diagnostic tools, and authentic OEM accessories to restore your devices to factory specifications.

Diagnostic Troubleshooting

Common Hearing Aid Problems We Fix in Clinic

Dead / Silent Hearing Aid

Clearing blocked wax guards, diagnosing battery contact corrosion, testing microphone ports, or replacing failed receivers.

Whistling / Feedback Noise

Eliminating squealing sounds by checking earmold seal integrity, clearing canal wax, or recalibrating feedback cancellation algorithms.

Distorted or Muffled Sound

Repairing cracked sound tubes, cleaning moisture buildup, and updating digital frequency response curves through audiometric fine-tuning.

Charging & Battery Issues

Troubleshooting rechargeable dock connections, battery drain anomalies, and replacing depleted lithium-ion cells.

Workshop Capabilities

Comprehensive Hearing Aid Maintenance Services

  • Deep Ultrasonic Chamber Cleaning: Sub-micron cavitation cleaning that safely removes hardened earwax, skin flakes, and microscopic debris without damaging sensitive acoustic transducers.
  • Electronic Acoustic Output Testing: Verifying that output decibel levels and frequency response curves match your prescribed audiogram settings using calibrated test boxes.
  • Industrial Vacuum Dehumidification: Removing deep internal moisture and humidity trapped within the casing using specialized vacuum drying chambers.
  • Receiver (Speaker) & Microphone Replacement: Replacing weak, intermittent, or buzzing speaker drivers and dual directional microphone assemblies with genuine manufacturer parts.
  • Custom Earmold Re-tubing & Resurfacing: Replacing stiff, discolored acoustic tubing, re-venting custom earmolds, and buffing acrylic or silicone shells for comfortable fit.
  • Computerized Acoustic Reprogramming & Fine-Tuning: Readjusting compression ratios, frequency gains, and noise reduction settings when patient hearing thresholds change.
Daily Care Guidance

Daily Preventive Care Tips from Our Audiologists

  1. Keep Them Dry: Always remove your hearing aids before bathing, swimming, or applying hairspray and perfume. Store them overnight in a desiccant drying container or charging case.
  2. Wipe Daily with a Dry Cloth: Clean the outer shell every evening with a soft dry microfiber cloth. Never use water, alcohol, or household cleaning detergents.
  3. Inspect Wax Guards Regularly: Check the white wax filter at the tip of the speaker dome. If discolored with wax, replace it using your wax-tool kit.
  4. Schedule Periodic Professional Servicing: Bring your devices to SoundBridge every 4 to 6 months for professional deep cleaning and acoustic verification.
